Athlete EMG — Privacy Policy

Last updated: June 2026  ·  SafeStrongSport, 501(c)(3) non-profit, EIN 99-2750707

Overview

Athlete EMG is a mobile app for monitoring forearm muscle fatigue during tennis sessions using a Bluetooth EMG/IMU wearable sensor. It is developed and maintained by SafeStrongSport.

Data we collect

The app reads EMG (electromyography) and IMU (accelerometer/gyroscope) data from a Bluetooth LE wearable device you wear on your forearm. This data is processed on your device to compute forearm muscle fatigue metrics. No personal health identifiers are collected.

How data is stored

All sensor data and session history (duration, swing count, fatigue level) are stored locally on your device only, in app-private storage. This data is never transmitted to any server, cloud service, or third party.

Bluetooth permissions

The app requires Bluetooth permissions to scan for and connect to your EMG sensor:

  • BLUETOOTH_SCAN / BLUETOOTH_CONNECT (Android 12+) — to find and connect to the sensor.
  • ACCESS_FINE_LOCATION (Android 11 and earlier) — required by Android to perform Bluetooth scans; the app does not use your location for any other purpose.

These permissions are used solely for sensor connectivity. No location data is stored or transmitted.

Demo Mode

Demo Mode replays a pre-recorded session bundled with the app. No sensor or network connection is required. No data is collected during Demo Mode.

Children

This app is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from children.

Third-party libraries

The app uses the following open-source libraries, none of which collect or transmit personal data: FastBLE (Bluetooth LE), Chaquopy (Python runtime), AndroidX.

Changes to this policy

We may update this policy from time to time. The current version will always be available at this URL and within the app. Continued use of the app after changes constitutes acceptance of the updated policy.
